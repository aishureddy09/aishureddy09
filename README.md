## About me

I am an MSc Artificial Intelligence with Healthcare student at Aston University (Sep 2025 – Sep 2026), with a BTech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science from Jansons Institute of Technology (2021 – 2025).

My dissertation applies deep learning to dermatological image analysis, working with the HAM10000 dataset (10,015 dermoscopic images, seven diagnostic classes) — building a custom CNN and a MobileNetV2 transfer-learning model in TensorFlow/Keras, and tackling the problems that decide whether a medical AI system is actually trustworthy: class imbalance, per-class sensitivity and specificity, explainability, and the limits of retrospective public data.

My portfolio research extends that dissertation in three connected directions:

- **Evaluation** — bias, fairness and reliability auditing of skin-lesion classifiers
- **Governance** — a risk-assessment and assurance framework for healthcare AI
- **Method** — a genuine U-Net implementation for medical image segmentation

Before my MSc I developed mobile applications as a Flutter Developer Intern at ELGI Equipments Limited and an Android Development Intern at CodeClause, and I have led data-annotation and web-development project teams — hands-on experience of the data labelling and delivery work that sits underneath machine-learning systems.

My research interest, and the direction of my GovAI UK Summer Fellowship 2027 application, is trustworthy AI in high-stakes settings: how AI systems are evaluated, where they fail, who they fail for, and what evidence and oversight should be required before deployment.

## Featured projects

| Project | What it demonstrates | Links |
|---|---|---|
| **Bias, Fairness and Reliability Evaluation of Skin-Lesion AI** | Extends my dissertation beyond headline accuracy: dataset audit, leakage-aware splits, per-class precision/recall/F1, sensitivity and specificity, a controlled class-weighting experiment, and subgroup reporting with Wilson intervals only where metadata genuinely supports it. On synthetic demo data, class weighting lifted macro-F1 from 0.417 to 0.628 | [Repository](https://github.com/aishureddy09/skin-lesion-bias-fairness-evaluation) |
| **Healthcare AI Governance and Risk Assessment Framework** | A lifecycle-based risk register and assurance framework for AI-assisted skin-lesion triage: stakeholder mapping, a risk taxonomy covering data quality, representativeness, false negatives, automation bias, privacy and drift, pre-deployment evidence requirements, post-deployment monitoring, an assurance checklist and a short policy brief. Desk-research framework — not a validated standard or legal advice | [Repository](https://github.com/aishureddy09/healthcare-ai-governance-framework) |
| **U-Net for Skin-Lesion Segmentation** | A hands-on pixel-level segmentation pipeline: image–mask preparation, a genuine U-Net implemented from scratch in pure NumPy, Dice/IoU evaluation (test Dice 0.990 on synthetic data), overlay visualisations and failure-mode analysis. Practical evidence beyond my classification-focused dissertation | [Repository](https://github.com/aishureddy09/unet-skin-lesion-segmentation) |
